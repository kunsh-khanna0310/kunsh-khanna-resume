About Me

I am currently pursuing B.Com (Hons.) at Ramjas College, University of Delhi, alongside the BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship programme at IIM Bangalore. My academic interests lie in business, finance and entrepreneurship, and I am keen to understand how classroom learning connects with practical experiences.

Outside academics, wildlife photography has been a major part of my life for over six years. I enjoy observing wildlife and using photographs to tell stories about the natural world. My work has been featured by Nature inFocus, Amateur Photographer Magazine (UK) and Digital Photographer Magazine.

Career Objectives

I hope to build a strong foundation in business, finance and entrepreneurship while gaining experience beyond the classroom. I am interested in learning from people with different perspectives, taking on responsibilities through projects and exploring how ideas move from planning to execution. Over time, I would like to use these experiences to identify an area I care about and work towards building something of my own.
